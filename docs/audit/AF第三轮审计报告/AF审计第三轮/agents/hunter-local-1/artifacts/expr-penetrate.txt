### 证据3 min/max 混类型 TypeError 穿透 executor ###
=== [1] 关键前提：_as_num 拒绝所有字符串（含数字串）===
  _as_num('21.5') -> ExprError: t() 需要数字，收到 '21.5'（实体状态请声明 type=numeric）
  _as_num(21.5) -> 21.5

=== [2] 因此 min/max 多参数形式会直接比较原始值 ===
  实体状态（未声明 type=numeric）→ 字符串；数字字面量 → 数字
  min(['21.5', 20]) -> ❌ TypeError: '<' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'
  max(['21.5', 20]) -> ❌ TypeError: '>' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'
  min([20, '21.5']) -> ❌ TypeError: '<' not supported between instances of 'str' and 'int'
  max([20, '21.5']) -> ❌ TypeError: '>' not supported between instances of 'str' and 'int'
  min(['on', 1]) -> ❌ TypeError: '<' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'
  max(['on', 1]) -> ❌ TypeError: '>' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'
  min(['unavailable', 0]) -> ❌ TypeError: '<' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'
  max(['unavailable', 0]) -> ❌ TypeError: '>' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'

=== [3] 单数组形式（len(args)==1）同样路径 ===
  min([['21.5', 20]]) -> ❌ TypeError: '<' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'
  min([['on', 1]]) -> ❌ TypeError: '<' not supported between instances of 'int' and 'str'

=== [4] floor/ceil 的 inf/nan：可达性重估 ===
  _as_num 拒绝 'inf'/'nan' 字符串 → 从 HA 实体状态字符串路径【不可达】
  仅当操作数已是 float 且为 inf/nan 时才触发
  实测：
    floor(inf) -> ❌ ValueError: cannot convert float NaN to integer
    ceil(inf) -> ❌ ValueError: cannot convert float NaN to integer
    floor(nan) -> ❌ ValueError: cannot convert float NaN to integer
    ceil(nan) -> ❌ ValueError: cannot convert float NaN to integer

=== [5] 结论 ===
  A. min/max 混类型 → TypeError ❌ 未受控（真实可达：实体状态字符串 vs 数字字面量）
  B. floor/ceil inf/nan → ValueError（可达性受限：需操作数已是 float inf/nan）
  C. af_executor.py:402 捕获 (UnknownEntity, ExprError, KeyError, ValueError)
     → TypeError 不在其中！A 会穿透 _soft_fail
